# We shipped the wrong pricing page for six weeks

By Marcus Webb

Last year I made the mistake of trusting our analytics dashboard over our support inbox. My first month at this company, I'd rebuilt the pricing page around what the numbers said visitors wanted. The numbers were wrong, and it took six weeks and two lost deals to find out.

Here's what happened. A prospect named Dana asked on a call why we charged per seat when her team rotates through contractors. I told her it was a legacy thing from our early days. She paused, then asked the question I still think about: "Who actually pays you per seat?" Nobody, it turned out. Agencies paid us, and agencies staff flexibly.

We changed the pricing page that Friday. Sign-ups from agency traffic went up 30% in the following month, up from roughly flat before. The dashboard hadn't lied exactly, it just counted the visitors who clicked, not the ones who left confused.

If you're staring at a conversion metric that won't move, maybe check the inbox first. Support tickets are slow, messy, and full of sentences no heatmap will ever show you. I suspect most teams already know this and, like me, keep not doing it.
