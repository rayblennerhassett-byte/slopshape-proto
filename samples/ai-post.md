# How to Cut Onboarding Time in Half: A Complete Guide

In this post, we'll cover why onboarding stalls, three fixes that work, and how to measure the difference.

## Why Onboarding Stalls

Employee onboarding is crucial for retention. Studies show that a structured onboarding process improves new-hire productivity. Furthermore, a robust program can unlock the full potential of your team and seamlessly integrate new employees into your company culture.

Delve into any HR survey and you'll find that manual onboarding is a game-changer gone wrong. In today's fast-paced business environment, it's important to note that delays cost money. The cost of getting this wrong compounds every quarter you stay manual.

## Three Fixes That Work

First, automate your paperwork. Unlike the old way of paper forms and email chains, modern platforms leverage templates to elevate the experience. Second, assign a buddy. Moreover, this simple step is pivotal for engagement. Third, track progress in a checklist so nothing falls through the cracks.

## How to Measure the Difference

Track time-to-first-task compared to last quarter. Companies utilizing these strategies see remarkable improvements. Furthermore, a vast array of tools can help you navigate the landscape of onboarding software.

## Key Takeaways

In conclusion, structured onboarding saves time. By leveraging automation, buddy systems, and checklists, you can cut onboarding time in half and boost retention across your entire organization.
