# How to Cut Bread Waste in Half: A Complete Guide

In this post, we'll cover why bakeries waste bread, three fixes that work, and how to measure the difference.

## Why Bakeries Waste Bread

Bread waste is crucial for margins. Studies show that structured baking processes reduce unsold inventory. Furthermore, a robust waste program can unlock the full potential of your production line and seamlessly integrate inventory tracking into your bakery operations.

Delve into any food-service survey and you'll find that manual ordering is a game-changer gone wrong. In today's fast-paced business environment, it's important to note that overproduction compounds every quarter you stay manual. The cost of getting this wrong is wasted flour, wasted labor, and wasted shelf space.

## Three Fixes That Work

First, automate your ordering. Unlike the old way of clipboards and guesswork, modern platforms leverage data to elevate the experience. Second, bake to demand. Moreover, this step is pivotal for freshness. Third, track waste in a checklist so nothing falls through the cracks.

## How to Measure the Difference

Track unsold loaves compared to last quarter. Companies utilizing these strategies see remarkable improvements. Furthermore, a vast array of tools can help you navigate the landscape of bakery software.

## Key Takeaways

In conclusion, structured baking saves money. By leveraging automation, demand-based production, and checklists, you can cut bread waste in half and boost margins across your entire operation.
